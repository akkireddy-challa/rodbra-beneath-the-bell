# Game design — RÖDBRÅ: Beneath the Bell

## Pitch

Winter, 1893. Monster hunter Liv Ravn returns to the isolated Scandinavian valley of Vargdal after every church bell rings simultaneously, despite their clappers having been removed. Nameless dead villagers rise from frozen ground. Liv searches for her missing sister Elin and discovers that Elin has been kept alive beneath the church as the human voice sealing an ancient root-bound entity called the Bell Mother. A browser-playable third-person dark-fantasy action slasher featuring responsive three-hit combos, charged heavy attacks, dodge invulnerability, directional parries, iron ward projectiles, checkpoints at iron prayer posts, Rend executions, four distinct enemy archetypes, two minibosses, and a 3-phase final boss fight beneath a colossal suspended cracked bronze bell, culminating in a poignant story choice and dawn endings.

## Core loop

- **Traverse & Explore:** Journey through four interconnected atmospheric zones (The Last Ring, Hushwood Approach, The Red Mill, Below the Bell) guided by blood-red ritual ribbons, lanterns, silhouettes, and distant bell tolls.
- **Combat & Stagger:** Engage thralls and guardians using responsive sword strikes (light 3-hit combo, charged heavy thrust, sprint and dodge slashes). Time directional parries to deflect agile strikes and create riposte openings.
- **Resource Management & Rend Executions:** Manage 3 rechargeable iron wards and checkpoint-refilled health vials. Land consecutive strikes to fill the Rend meter, spending it on cinematic close-up executions against staggered monsters.
- **Upgrade & Prepare:** Scavenge iron nails from fallen foes and hidden caches to forge one of three permanent prayer upgrades (Tempered Edge, Woven Charm, Quickened Ward) at iron prayer posts.
- **Unravel the Rite:** Breaching the subterranean root sanctuary, confront the Bell Mother across 3 escalating phases, rescue or relieve Elin, and choose the fate of Vargdal.

## World & look

Setting: The remote Scandinavian mountain valley of Vargdal in the deep winter of 1893.
Palette: Charcoal weathered timber and basalt rock (#0E0C0B), crisp snow white (#FFFFFF / #DDE5EC), moonlit cold blue dusk (#1E2838), oxidized bronze (#3C5A52), and deep blood-red crimson accents (#9C1C1C).
Atmosphere: Cold drifting volumetric snow flurries, distant burning bell tower, red ritual ribbons fluttering on frosted pine boughs, frozen riverbanks beneath an abandoned sawmill waterwheel, and ancient root-choked stone vaults beneath the ruined stave church where a gigantic cracked bronze bell hangs suspended by iron chains.
Soundscape: Bowed nyckelharpa and cello bass drones, hollow frame drum pulses, breathy nonverbal choral laments, cold whistling mountain wind, iron metal clangs, and deep resonance of bronze church bells.

## Art direction

Original Nordic folk-horror action slasher with clear, readable game silhouettes, dark textured materials, and expressive lighting. Ash-blonde braided hair, weathered charcoal greatcoat with blood-stained hem, iron arm guards, seax-style broad blade, and iron talisman ward. Enemies feature readable silhouettes: gaunt cowl-wrapped Hollow Thralls, tall bone-and-antler Wardens, heavy hooded executioner Mill Butchers with twin cleavers, and root-entangled Bellbound priestesses. Interface follows charcoal wood, ivory serif typography, oxidized iron framing, and deep crimson highlights.

## Win / lose

- **Win:** Defeat the Bell Mother through all 3 combat phases, approach the altar of the Bell, make the fateful choice between breaking the seal or offering Liv's blood, witness the corresponding dawn ending cutscene and credits.
- **Lose:** Health drops to 0. The screen fades to dark charcoal with the crimson epitaph "THE BELL REMEMBERS" and bells tolling. The player respawns at the most recent iron prayer post checkpoint with collected upgrades preserved.
